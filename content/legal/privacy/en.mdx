## In short

- No advertising and no cross-site tracking. No Google Analytics, no social-media pixels, no third-party scripts.
- One cookie, `marcfors-locale`, written only when you pick a language. Your language and theme choices also stay in your browser's localStorage. None of these identifies you.
- That is why there is no cookie banner: the law asks for consent only for cookies that are not strictly necessary or that track you.
- The site processes very little personal data: the server logs needed to run it, performance measurements without identifiers, and whatever you send me by e-mail or through a tip on Stripe.

## What is processed, and why

**Serving the pages.** When you load a page, the hosting provider, Vercel Inc. (USA), receives your IP address, user agent and the URL requested, as any web server does. These request logs exist to deliver the site, defend it against abuse and debug failures. Vercel keeps them briefly; the site itself stores none. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)).

**Rate limiting.** The two beacon endpoints below count requests per IP address in memory for one minute, then forget them. Nothing is written to disk.

**Performance measurements (web vitals).** Your browser sends five timing metrics (LCP, INP, CLS, FCP, TTFB) to `/api/vitals`. Each report carries the metric name, its value and a random id for that page load, and nothing else: no IP address, no cookie, no account. They are logged so I can keep the site fast. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

**Audience measurement.** Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies or persistent identifiers. It derives a hash from the request that is discarded within 24 hours, so visits cannot be linked to you across days, and I only ever see aggregate counts. Legal basis: legitimate interest. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

**Error reports.** If a page crashes, the browser sends the error message and a digest to `/api/errors` so I can fix it. No personal data is included on purpose. Legal basis: legitimate interest.

**E-mail.** If you write to developer@marcfors.com, I keep your message and address for as long as the conversation, and any work that follows from it, needs them. Legal basis: steps prior to a contract at your request (art. 6(1)(b)) or legitimate interest.

**Tips (Support · 1,99 €).** The support button sends you to a payment page operated by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland, and Stripe Inc., USA). Stripe collects your card details and e-mail address under [its own privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy); I never see the card number. Stripe shows me the amount, the date, your e-mail address and the project you were tipping from. I keep that record for as long as Spanish tax and accounting law requires, up to six years. Legal basis: performance of the transaction (art. 6(1)(b)) and legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).

## Cookies and browser storage

- **`marcfors-locale`** (first-party cookie, one year): set only when you pick a language in the switcher, so the site reopens in that language.
- **`marcfors-locale`** (localStorage): the same choice, kept on the client until you clear it.
- **`marcfors-theme`** (localStorage): the colour palette you picked, kept until you clear it.

These remember a choice you made yourself, which exempts them from consent under art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive and the cookie guide of the Spanish data protection authority. You can delete them at any time from your browser's settings; the site then simply follows your browser's language and your system theme. The Stripe payment page sets its own cookies under Stripe's policy.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)), or the authority of your own EU country.
