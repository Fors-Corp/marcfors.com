## Problem

Personal metals, crypto and portfolio positions needed cost basis and alerts without sending holdings to a multi-tenant SaaS.

## Approach

A single-user Next.js app on Prisma and SQLite tracks metals, crypto and portfolio rows, raises alerts, and can optionally import from Revolut X.

An Ask side chat answers questions about the whole page in English or Spanish. The server indexes a snapshot of every section — net worth, holdings, prices, alerts and every chart range — in my embedded-rag; Claude answers only from it, and each source chip scrolls to the card it cites. A wide golden eval checks citations, numbers and abstains.

## Result

A private finance desk for one operator — no public source link.
