## Problem

A personal site that recruiters actually scan needed to prove engineering taste without advertising or cross-site analytics, cookie banners or a fragile CMS.

## Approach

The desk is a Next.js App Router portfolio: Spectral and Manrope, CSS tokens, seven URL locales, first-party Core Web Vitals, a dependency audit on every build, and case studies as MDX. The public mailbox is the only contact on the page.

## Result

A live observatory desk whose source is public under Fors-Corp, with privacy and security headers treated as product features.
