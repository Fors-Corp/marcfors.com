## Problem

Weekly planning tools either ignore the hours you are already busy or dump every habit into a flat checklist. Neither survives a real week.

## Approach

Day templates, busy intervals, and push reminders in Spanish and English, plus a public marketplace of routines. Built on Next.js, Clerk, Neon and Drizzle.

## Result

A live product a recruiter can click. Source stays private; the app is the proof.
