# Fors Corp

## Problem

Fors Corp needed a public face that sold websites and AI assistants to Barcelona clinics and advisory firms without cookies, analytics or a heavy CMS.

## Approach

forscorp.es is statically prerendered in Spanish, Catalan and English on the Fors design system. The copy explains the offer; the stack stays deliberately thin so the storefront matches the privacy posture of the products it sells.

## Result

A live company site that demonstrates the same constraints the consultancy ships to clients, with the implementation kept private.
