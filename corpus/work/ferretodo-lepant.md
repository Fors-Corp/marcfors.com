# FerreTodo Lepant

## Problem

A neighbourhood hardware store on Carrer de Lepant needed a page locals and visitors could use without loading a map or chat widget up front.

## Approach

A single Next.js page in Spanish, Catalan, English and French: live open-now status, WhatsApp contact, light and dark themes drawn from the shop sign, and a map that loads only when requested.

## Result

A live storefront that answers hours and contact first, with private source and no tracking stack.
