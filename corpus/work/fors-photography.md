# Fors Photography

## Problem

I wanted a storefront for my own photography that sold signed prints without building accounts, inventory software or a tracking stack around a small catalogue.

## Approach

forsphoto.com is a Next.js shop with 18 signed fine-art prints in four collections and four sizes, copy in six languages, and Stripe-hosted checkout. There is no product database, customer accounts or analytics layer — the catalogue and locales live with the app.

## Result

A live print shop that takes orders through Stripe while staying small enough to maintain alone, with the source kept private.
